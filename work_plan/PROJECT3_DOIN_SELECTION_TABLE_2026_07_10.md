## Current Reading

- Full-year evidence remains the primary selector for the first DOIN plugin/config pass.
- Partial/rush evidence is useful as a search seed, especially for portfolio opportunity detection, but should not replace full-year evidence without completion.
- The current data is still mostly SAC actor-critic with fixed/default hyperparameters; negative RAP does not eliminate an asset from DOIN exploration because the optimizer has not tuned it yet.
- Short horizon and long horizon should be selected separately because trade frequency, input significance, and risk geometry differ.
